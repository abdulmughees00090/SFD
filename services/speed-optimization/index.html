Why Page Speed Matters For SEO & Sales

Over 53% of mobile users abandon sites that take longer than 3 seconds to load. Google also uses Core Web Vitals as a direct search ranking signal. We diagnose and eliminate performance bottlenecks across your entire stack.

How We Speed Up Your Site

  • Core Web Vitals Repair: Fix Largest Contentful Paint (LCP), Interaction to Next Paint (INP), and Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS).
  • Asset Minification & Deferral: Minify CSS/JS files, defer non-critical render-blocking JavaScript.
  • Next-Gen Image Formatting: Compress images to WebP/AVIF formats with responsive srcset attributes.
  • Server & Caching Tuning: Configure CDN caching, Gzip/Brotli compression, and database query optimization.
