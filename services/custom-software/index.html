Frequently Asked Questions

What types of custom software systems do you develop?

We build custom Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) systems, Cloud POS dashboards, inventory management portals, booking SaaS applications, and custom business automation tools.

Can custom software be integrated with our existing databases or APIs?

Yes, we engineer custom REST/GraphQL APIs and database connectors to integrate with third-party software, payment gateways, and legacy business databases.

Is custom software hosted on our servers or in the cloud?

We deploy software according to your requirements, including AWS, Google Cloud, digital cloud VPS, or private on-premise infrastructure.
