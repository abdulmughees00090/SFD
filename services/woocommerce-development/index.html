Frequently Asked Questions

Why choose WooCommerce over SaaS e-commerce platforms?

WooCommerce provides full ownership of your store data, zero per-transaction platform fees, and infinite customization potential for custom payment gateways, local currencies, and custom checkout flows.

Can you integrate regional payment gateways like M-Pesa, Paystack, or JazzCash?

Yes. We integrate local payment gateways including Stripe, PayPal, Paystack, M-Pesa, Flutterwave, JazzCash, EasyPaisa, and custom Cash on Delivery (COD) extensions.

Do you provide ongoing WooCommerce maintenance and updates?

Yes, we offer monthly WooCommerce care plans covering core, theme, and plugin updates, automated offsite backups, database optimization, and real-time security monitoring.
