## 6. Risk Assessment & Mitigations

1. **Risk**: Thin content penalty on market/country pages.
   - *Mitigation*: Ensure every country page has 100% unique, factual content detailing local payment gateways (M-Pesa, Paystack, GCash, etc.), network constraints, and regional business habits. No duplicate templates.
2. **Risk**: Over-promising physical presence.
   - *Mitigation*: Strictly maintain "remote-first global software agency" positioning. Highlight remote collaboration, transparent time-zone alignment, and online communication.
