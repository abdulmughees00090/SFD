Commission-Based Sales Agent (Software Products)

Commission Only Field-Based Multiple Positions

Sell Silver Fox Dynamics software products in your own city and earn a recurring commission every time your client pays, until the client goes live. Work your own hours, with your own agent portal.

About the role

Silver Fox Dynamics is expanding the reach of its software products across Pakistan and is registering sales agents across Pakistan, with a focus on Lahore, Islamabad, Karachi, Faisalabad and Multan. This is not an office job and it has no basic salary: you are rewarded purely on the business you bring in.

You find the businesses, pitch our products, and stay with your client through to go-live. Every time your client pays, you earn your commission, and you can track it all from your own agent portal.

Responsibilities

  • Identify and approach businesses in your city that can benefit from our software, such as restaurants, event venues, retailers, SMEs and enterprises needing ERP or custom systems.
  • Introduce and pitch Silver Fox Dynamics products and services, and arrange demos or meetings with our team where needed.
  • Register each of your clients in the agent portal and keep their details up to date.
  • Follow up consistently and guide the client from first conversation through payment and go-live.
  • Represent Silver Fox Dynamics honestly and professionally, with no false promises to clients.
  • Track your clients and commissions in the portal and submit withdrawal requests when you are ready.

Requirements

  • Based in, and familiar with the business market of, your city in Pakistan, especially Lahore, Islamabad, Karachi, Faisalabad or Multan.
  • Confident communicator in Urdu and/or English (Punjabi or Sindhi is a plus) who can hold a convincing business conversation.
  • Self-motivated and disciplined: you work without supervision and follow up without being chased.
  • Your own transport, smartphone and internet access for field visits and the agent portal.
  • Honesty and integrity in dealing with clients and with the company.
  • Prior B2B, field-sales, IT or software-reselling experience is a plus; passionate beginners with a strong local network are welcome.

Nice to have

  • An existing network of business owners, shop-owners, restaurateurs or venue managers.
  • Experience selling software, IT services or other subscription products.
  • Experience as a freelance or independent sales representative.

What we offer

  • Commission-only earnings: no basic salary and no earnings cap.
  • Recurring commission: whenever your client pays, you receive your commission, until the client is live.
  • A personal agent portal where you can view your current commission, submit withdrawal requests and manage your clients.
  • Fuel allowance on successful sales, provided at the company’s discretion once you have shown seriousness and results.
  • Full commission terms are discussed with shortlisted agents before registration. Income depends entirely on your sales and is not guaranteed.

How to apply

Submit your application through the official application form. Applications sent via WhatsApp, direct email or social media are not considered, and only shortlisted candidates are contacted. Read the application guidelines.
