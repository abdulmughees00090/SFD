Junior Oracle APEX Developer

Full-Time Lahore On-site

Start your career on real Oracle APEX projects. Build pages, forms and reports under senior guidance, write SQL and PL/SQL, and grow into a full APEX developer.

About the role

We are looking for a motivated Junior Oracle APEX Developer to work in Lahore. You will sit at our client's premises near Bahria Town, report to our head office in Islamabad, and work alongside our senior APEX developer on live client projects, learning how professional APEX applications are designed, built, tested and supported.

Fresh graduates and developers with up to one year of experience are welcome. What matters most is your grip on database fundamentals and your appetite to learn quickly.

Responsibilities

  • Build and modify APEX pages, forms, interactive reports and dashboards under the guidance of senior developers.
  • Write and test SQL queries and PL/SQL procedures, functions and packages.
  • Create and maintain database tables, views and sequences as per the design.
  • Fix bugs, handle change requests and support testing and user acceptance.
  • Prepare simple technical documentation and release notes.
  • Follow team coding standards and take part in code reviews.
  • Keep learning: APEX features, ORDS and REST services, and Oracle database best practice.

Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology or a related field.
  • Fresher or up to 1 year of experience (internships and training count).
  • Good understanding of relational databases and strong basic SQL (joins, sub-queries, aggregation).
  • Working knowledge of PL/SQL fundamentals.
  • Basic HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
  • Strong learning attitude, attention to detail and the ability to take feedback.
  • Able to work on-site at a client's premises in Lahore (near Bahria Town); living nearby is preferred.

Nice to have

  • Hands-on exposure to Oracle APEX through a course, internship, FYP or personal project.
  • Oracle SQL or APEX training or certification.
  • Familiarity with Git.

What we offer

  • Salary: PKR 80,000 – 100,000 per month, based on skills and interview performance.
  • Hands-on mentoring from senior Oracle APEX developers.
  • Real client work from week one, with a clear path to a mid-level APEX role.

How to apply

Submit your application through the official application form. Applications sent via WhatsApp, direct email or social media are not considered, and only shortlisted candidates are contacted. Read the application guidelines.
