Senior Oracle APEX Developer

Full-Time Lahore On-site

Own the design and delivery of enterprise Oracle APEX applications on long-term, client-based engagements. You will lead technically, write production-grade PL/SQL, and mentor our junior APEX developers.

About the role

Silver Fox Dynamics builds ERP, SaaS and business-automation software for clients across industries, and Oracle APEX is at the heart of several of our production systems, including our cloud Restaurant ERP. We are hiring a Senior Oracle APEX Developer to take technical ownership of a long-term, client-based engagement in Lahore. You will sit at our client's premises near Bahria Town, Lahore, and report to our head office in Islamabad.

This is a hands-on senior role. You will design the solution, build the hardest parts yourself, review the work of others, and be the person clients trust to get complex requirements delivered properly.

Responsibilities

  • Design, build and maintain end-to-end Oracle APEX applications: interactive reports and grids, forms, dashboards, charts and approval workflows.
  • Write, optimise and troubleshoot advanced SQL and PL/SQL (packages, procedures, functions, triggers, views) on Oracle Database.
  • Model relational schemas and own data-integrity, indexing and performance decisions.
  • Build and consume REST services with Oracle REST Data Services (ORDS) and integrate third-party APIs and payment, messaging or accounting systems.
  • Implement authentication, authorisation schemes and role-based access, and apply secure-coding practice (SQL injection, XSS, session handling).
  • Translate client requirements into technical designs, estimates and delivery plans, and take part in client meetings, demos and UAT.
  • Review code, set APEX and PL/SQL standards, and mentor junior developers.
  • Manage environments and releases (development, testing, production) using version control and repeatable deployment practice.
  • Provide go-live and post-launch support, and keep technical documentation current.

Requirements

  • 9–10 years of hands-on professional experience building and delivering Oracle APEX applications to production.
  • Strong command of SQL and PL/SQL, including query tuning with execution plans.
  • Deep working knowledge of APEX: Page Designer, Interactive Grid / Report, Dynamic Actions, Universal Theme, template components, plug-ins and shared components.
  • Practical experience with ORDS and REST web services (JSON).
  • Solid JavaScript, HTML5 and CSS for customising APEX UI.
  • Experience with Git or equivalent version control.
  • Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, and clear written and spoken communication for client-facing work.
  • Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology or a related field.

Nice to have

  • Oracle Cloud / Autonomous Database experience.
  • Oracle certifications (APEX Developer Professional, SQL / PL/SQL).
  • BI Publisher or other PDF/report generation experience.
  • Background in ERP, POS, inventory or finance applications.
  • Experience leading or mentoring a small development team.

What we offer

  • Salary: PKR 220,000 – 250,000 per month (gross), based on experience and interview performance.
  • Long-term engagement on real client projects and live production systems.
  • Technical ownership and a genuine voice in how we build.
  • Based in Lahore, near Bahria Town, at the client's premises, reporting to our Islamabad head office. Candidates living near Bahria Town will be preferred.

How to apply

Submit your application through the official application form. Applications sent via WhatsApp, direct email or social media are not considered, and only shortlisted candidates are contacted. Read the application guidelines.
