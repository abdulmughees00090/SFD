How to Accept Mobile Money & Online Payments on Your Website

Published by Silver Fox Dynamics Engineering Team | October 2026

Expanding online sales in emerging markets requires supporting local payment methods like M-Pesa, Paystack, MTN MoMo, and regional card processors alongside international options.

Published: October 2026 Category: Payments & E-Commerce Engineering Guide
1

Selecting the Right Integration Architecture

Choose between hosted checkout redirects and embedded inline payment forms based on your PCI-DSS compliance requirements and technical capabilities.

2

Managing Webhooks & Order Confirmation Security

Ensure payment status verification happens server-to-server via cryptographic signatures before fulfilling orders to prevent fraud and transaction spoofing.

3

Fallback Workflows & Cash-On-Delivery (COD) Options

Provide automated SMS or email order receipts with manual bank transfer instructions or COD verification for customers hesitant to pay upfront.
