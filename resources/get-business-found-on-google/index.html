How to Get Your Business Found on Google: Local & Technical SEO

Published by Silver Fox Dynamics Engineering Team | October 2026

Ranking on Google requires a combination of search engine crawlability, high page performance, structured data, and content targeted specifically to commercial search intent.

1. Technical SEO Foundation

Ensure Googlebot can discover and index your pages without obstacles.

  • Fix robots.txt so CSS and JavaScript resources are not blocked.
  • Ensure every page has a unique Title, Meta Description, canonical URL tag, and clean H1 hierarchy.
  • Submit a valid sitemap.xml in Google Search Console.

2. Commercial Search-Intent Content

Target transactional and decision-stage keywords rather than generic informative terms. Create dedicated landing pages for specific service offerings, location hubs, and industry use-cases.

3. Structured Data & Entity Clarity

Implement Schema.org JSON-LD structured data (Organization, WebSite, Service, FAQPage) so search engine crawlers and AI search agents (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity) clearly understand your business entity and services.
