Frequently Asked Questions

Which Bangladeshi Mobile Financial Services (MFS) do you integrate?

We integrate bKash MFS tokenized checkout, Nagad, Rocket (DBBL), SSLCommerz, and Shurjopay for seamless BDT transactions.

Can you migrate F-commerce (Facebook) businesses to custom web stores?

Yes. We convert Facebook-based social stores into automated WooCommerce or custom web storefronts with product catalogs, automated stock sync, and courier delivery webhooks.

Do you support Cash-on-Delivery (COD) and advance MFS deposit workflows?

Yes. We configure hybrid checkout solutions allowing Bangladeshi merchants to collect partial advance deposits via bKash/Nagad while reserving the balance for Cash-on-Delivery.
