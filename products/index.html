On-Demand

AI/ML Development

Harness the power of artificial intelligence and machine learning — without the overhead of building an in-house data science team. We provide on-demand AI/ML development services tailored to your specific business needs. From predictive analytics to LLM integration, we turn your data into actionable intelligence.

What we can build for you

  • Predictive Analytics — Forecast sales, demand, stock levels, or customer behavior
  • LLM & OpenAI Integration — Add ChatGPT-like capabilities, document Q&A, chatbots, and content generation to your apps
  • Custom ML Models — Classification, regression, clustering, and recommendation engines
  • Data Processing & Automation — Clean, transform, and automate repetitive tasks with AI
  • Computer Vision — Object detection, OCR enhancement, document analysis
  • AI Consulting & Strategy — Not sure where AI fits your business? We'll help you figure it out.

How on-demand AI/ML works

We don't believe in "black box" AI. Every project starts with understanding your data and goals. You pay for what you need — whether it's a one-off model, a full integration, or ongoing AI support.

  • ✅ No long-term commitment — project-based or retainer
  • ✅ You own the model and code — complete IP transfer
  • ✅ Deployment anywhere — cloud, on-premise, or edge
  • ✅ Transparent pricing — fixed scope or hourly
