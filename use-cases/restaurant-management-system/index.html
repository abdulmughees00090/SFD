Restaurant Management System Specifications

In-depth look at modules, hardware support, operational workflows, and system requirements.

Core Modules & Feature Breakdown

Our restaurant software architecture integrates front-of-house and kitchen operations:

  • Point of Sale (POS): Quick order creation, table mapping, bill splitting, and receipt generation.
  • Kitchen Display System (KDS): Real-time ticket routing by preparation station with order timer alerts.
  • Inventory & Recipe Tracking: Ingredient deduction per order, low-stock warnings, and purchase orders.
  • Menu Management: Multi-category pricing, modifier lists (add-ons, spice levels), and seasonal toggles.
  • Reports & Analytics: Daily sales summaries, top-selling items, and shift cashier reconciliation.

Target Food & Beverage Business Types

Engineered to adapt to diverse food service workflows:

  • Full-service dine-in restaurants requiring table status tracking and split checks.
  • Quick-service restaurants (QSR) and fast-food outlets needing rapid counter checkout.
  • Cafes and bakeries requiring customizable drink and food options.
  • Multi-location franchises needing centralized menu management across branches.

Implementation & Onboarding Workflow

How we deploy the system into your dining establishment:

  1. Menu & Tax Schema Setup: Import categories, prices, taxes, and modifier groups.
  2. Hardware Configuration: Pair thermal receipt printers, kitchen display tablets, and cash drawers.
  3. Role Assignment: Configure manager, cashier, server, and chef access privileges.
  4. Staff Training & Dry Run: Conduct test orders to ensure seamless shift transitions.

Hardware Requirements & Technical Limits

System prerequisites and operational boundaries:

  • Hardware Requirements: Standard touch-screen tablets or PCs running modern web browsers, ESC/POS thermal printers, local network router.
  • Technical Limits: Real-time cloud sync requires an active internet link; offline mode retains local sales data for sync once reconnected. Physical hardware maintenance is managed on-site.
