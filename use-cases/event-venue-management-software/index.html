All-In-One Venue & Event Operations

Managing physical venues and large-scale events requires seamless synchronization between venue calendars, ticket sales, gate access, and attendee records. EventOS delivers a unified web-based operating system designed to eliminate double-bookings and speed up event entry.

EventOS Operational Features

  • Real-Time Venue Scheduling: Prevent double-booking across halls, auditoriums, and outdoor spaces with real-time calendar locks.
  • Interactive Seating & Tiered Pricing: Configure custom venue maps, VIP seating blocks, and multi-tier pricing structures.
  • Digital Ticketing & Access Control: Issue instant digital passes with QR code validation for fast door check-ins.
  • Revenue & Attendance Analytics: Track ticket velocity, seat occupancy, and real-time revenue stats from a live dashboard.
